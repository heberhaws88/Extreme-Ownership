Tonight I want to talk about a principle from chapter twelve of Extreme Ownership by Jocko Willink and Leif Babin. The principle is this: discipline equals freedom. And the idea is simple but counterintuitive. The disciplines that feel restrictive in the moment — the routines, the structure, the standards, the things that seem like they slow you down or box you in — those are exactly what create the freedom, the trust, and the capability to handle whatever comes. When you have discipline baked in, when you've done the boring repetitive work ahead of time, you gain the freedom to move, to adapt, to lead, to think, because you're not spending all your energy just trying to keep the wheels on.
